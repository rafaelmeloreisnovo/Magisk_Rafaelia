## FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

RAFAELIA Framework is built upon inviolable ethical principles that prioritize:
1. **Human dignity and rights** (inalienable and universal)
2. **Child protection** (absolute and non-negotiable)
3. **Transparency and accountability** (in all operations)
4. **Safety and security** (for all users)
5. **Cultural respect** (multilingual, inclusive)
6. **Environmental responsibility** (sustainable computing)
7. **Accessibility** (universal design)
8. **Privacy protection** (data sovereignty)

## INTERNATIONAL LEGAL FRAMEWORKS

### United Nations (UN) Treaties and Conventions

#### 1. Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR, 1948)
- **Article 1**: All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights
- **Article 12**: No arbitrary interference with privacy, family, home or correspondence
- **Article 19**: Freedom of opinion and expression
- **Article 26**: Right to education
- **Application**: RAFAELIA ensures user rights, privacy, and freedom of expression

#### 2. Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC, 1989)
- **Article 3**: Best interests of the child as primary consideration
- **Article 16**: Protection from arbitrary interference with privacy
- **Article 17**: Access to appropriate information and material
- **Article 34**: Protection from all forms of sexual exploitation and abuse
- **Article 36**: Protection from all other forms of exploitation
- **Application**: RAFAELIA implements **MAXIMUM CHILD PROTECTION** (inegociável)

#### 3. International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR, 1966)
- **Article 17**: Right to privacy
- **Article 19**: Freedom of expression and information
- **Application**: Balance between openness and privacy protection

#### 4. International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (ICESCR, 1966)
- **Article 13**: Right to education
- **Article 15**: Right to participate in cultural life and benefit from scientific progress
- **Application**: Universal access to technology and knowledge

### UNESCO Frameworks

#### 1. Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence (2021)
- **Principle 1**: Proportionality and do no harm
- **Principle 2**: Safety and security
- **Principle 3**: Fairness and non-discrimination
- **Principle 4**: Sustainability
- **Principle 5**: Right to privacy and data protection
- **Principle 6**: Human oversight and determination
- **Principle 7**: Transparency and explainability
- **Principle 8**: Responsibility and accountability
- **Principle 9**: Awareness and literacy
- **Principle 10**: Multi-stakeholder and adaptive governance
- **Application**: RAFAELIA Ethica[8] implements all principles

#### 2. Universal Declaration on Cultural Diversity (2001)
- Cultural pluralism and multilingualism
- **Application**: 170-language support, Esperanto, Latin inclusion

## CHILD PROTECTION PROTOCOLS (Absolute Priority)

### Inegociável (Non-Negotiable) Requirements

1. **Zero tolerance** for content harmful to children
2. **Privacy by default** for all users under 18
3. **Age verification** where appropriate
4. **Content filtering** for age-appropriate access
5. **Parental controls** support
6. **Data minimization** for children
7. **No tracking** of children's activities
8. **No profiling** of children
9. **No advertising** targeting children
10. **Educational focus** in child-accessible features

### Compliance Standards
- **COPPA** (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, USA)
- **GDPR Article 8** (Child consent, EU)
- **LGPD Article 14** (Child data processing, Brazil)
- **UK Age Appropriate Design Code**
- **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - Minors**

## PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION

### Principles
- **Data minimization**: Collect only necessary data
- **Purpose limitation**: Use data only for stated purposes
- **Storage limitation**: Keep data only as long as needed
- **Accuracy**: Ensure data correctness
- **Integrity and confidentiality**: Protect data security
- **Accountability**: Demonstrate compliance

### Regulatory Compliance
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation, EU)
- **LGPD** (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, Brazil)
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act, USA)
- **PIPEDA** (Personal Information Protection, Canada)

## PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

RAFAELIA Framework **ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITS**:

1. ❌ Harm to human beings (especially children)
2. ❌ Invasion of privacy or unauthorized surveillance
3. ❌ Coercion or manipulation
4. ❌ Discrimination based on protected characteristics
5. ❌ Exploitation (especially of vulnerable populations)
6. ❌ Misinformation or deception
7. ❌ Unauthorized data collection
8. ❌ Malware or harmful code distribution
9. ❌ Copyright infringement or plagiarism
10. ❌ Circumvention of security measures for harmful purposes

## CONFLICT RESOLUTION

### Hierarchy of Norms
When ethical principles or legal requirements conflict:

1. **Most protective norm applies** (principle of maximum protection)
2. **Child protection takes precedence** over all other considerations
3. **Human rights supersede** efficiency or convenience
4. **Privacy favored** when balanced against other interests
5. **Transparency required** unless it endangers individuals

### Dispute Resolution
1. Good faith dialogue
2. Reference to established ethical frameworks
3. Community consultation
4. Independent ethics review
5. Legal compliance verification

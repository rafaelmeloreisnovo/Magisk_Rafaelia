## INTERNATIONAL TREATIES AND CONVENTIONS

### 1. UNITED NATIONS SYSTEM

#### Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR, 1948)
- **Status**: Customary international law
- **Relevance**: 
  - Article 12: Privacy rights
  - Article 19: Freedom of expression
  - Article 26: Right to education
  - Article 27: Cultural participation and scientific benefits
- **Application**: User rights protection in RAFAELIA

#### International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR, 1966)
- **Status**: Treaty (173 parties)
- **Key Articles**:
  - Article 17: Protection of privacy
  - Article 19: Freedom of expression
- **Application**: Privacy and expression balance

#### International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (ICESCR, 1966)
- **Status**: Treaty (171 parties)
- **Key Articles**:
  - Article 13: Right to education
  - Article 15: Cultural life and scientific progress
- **Application**: Educational access and cultural inclusivity

#### Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC, 1989)
- **Status**: Treaty (196 parties - universal except USA)
- **Key Articles**:
  - Article 3: Best interests of child
  - Article 16: Privacy protection
  - Article 17: Access to appropriate information
  - Article 34-36: Protection from exploitation
- **Application**: **MANDATORY** child protection in RAFAELIA

**Optional Protocols**:
- Sale of children, child prostitution, child pornography (2000)
- Children in armed conflict (2000)
- Communications procedure (2011)

### 2. WORLD INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ORGANIZATION (WIPO)

#### Berne Convention (1886, revised Paris 1971)
- **Status**: Treaty (181 parties)
- **Provisions**:
  - Article 2: Literary and artistic works protection
  - Article 5: Minimum standards (automatic protection)
  - Article 6bis: Moral rights (attribution, integrity)
  - Article 7: Protection duration (life + 50 years minimum)
- **Application**: Author attribution protection (Rafael Melo Reis Novo)

#### WIPO Copyright Treaty (WCT, 1996)
- **Status**: Treaty (113 parties)
- **Provisions**: Digital environment adaptations
- **Application**: Software copyright protection

#### WIPO Performances and Phonograms Treaty (WPPT, 1996)
- **Status**: Treaty (110 parties)
- **Application**: Multimedia content protection

### 3. COUNCIL OF EUROPE

#### European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR, 1950)
- **Status**: Treaty (46 parties)
- **Key Articles**:
  - Article 8: Right to privacy and family life
  - Article 10: Freedom of expression
- **Application**: European user rights

#### Convention on Cybercrime (Budapest Convention, 2001)
- **Status**: Treaty (68 parties worldwide)
- **Provisions**: Computer-related offenses, procedural law, international cooperation
- **Application**: Security practices, incident response

### 4. ORGANIZATION OF AMERICAN STATES (OAS)

#### American Convention on Human Rights (Pact of San José, 1969)
- **Status**: Treaty (25 parties)
- **Key Articles**:
  - Article 11: Privacy protection
  - Article 13: Freedom of thought and expression
- **Application**: Americas region compliance

### 5. AFRICAN UNION

#### African Charter on Human and Peoples' Rights (Banjul Charter, 1981)
- **Status**: Treaty (54 parties)
- **Provisions**: Individual and collective rights
- **Application**: African region compliance

#### African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child (1990)
- **Status**: Treaty (49 parties)
- **Application**: Child protection in African context

### 6. UNESCO INSTRUMENTS

## PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION

### EUROPEAN UNION

#### General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, 2016/679)
- **Scope**: EU/EEA residents
- **Key Requirements**:
  - Lawful basis for processing
  - Data subject rights (access, erasure, portability)
  - Privacy by design and by default
  - Data protection impact assessments
  - Data breach notification (72 hours)
  - DPO appointment (where required)
  - International transfer restrictions
- **Penalties**: Up to €20 million or 4% global turnover
- **Application**: EU user data protection

#### ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC, amended 2009)
- **Scope**: Electronic communications
- **Requirements**: Consent for cookies, confidentiality
- **Application**: Web services, communications

### BRAZIL

#### Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018)
- **Scope**: Brazilian residents
- **Key Requirements**: Similar to GDPR
  - Legal basis for processing
  - Data subject rights
  - Privacy by design
  - ANPD authority
- **Penalties**: Up to R$ 50 million or 2% revenue
- **Application**: Brazilian user data protection

### UNITED STATES

#### California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA, 2018) / CPRA (2020)
- **Scope**: California residents
- **Key Rights**: Know, delete, opt-out, correct
- **Application**: California user rights

#### Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 1998)
- **Scope**: Children under 13 (USA)
- **Requirements**: Parental consent, data minimization
- **Application**: **MANDATORY** for child users

#### Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA, 1996)
- **Scope**: Health information (USA)
- **Application**: If health data processed

### CANADA

#### Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA, 2000)
- **Scope**: Private sector (Canada)
- **Principles**: Fair information practices
- **Application**: Canadian user data

### CHINA

#### Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL, 2021)
- **Scope**: Chinese residents
- **Requirements**: Consent, data localization, impact assessments
- **Application**: Chinese user data

### OTHER JURISDICTIONS

- **Japan**: Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI, 2003)
- **South Korea**: Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA, 2011)
- **Australia**: Privacy Act 1988 (amended 2017)
- **Singapore**: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA, 2012)
- **India**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA, 2023)
